# Privacy policy

Fall Desktop does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- It has no accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry, and it makes no network connections.
- Its settings stay on your PC, in the Windows registry, and are removed when you
  uninstall it.
- The "Created by" link in the app opens the project page on GitHub in your web
  browser, where GitHub's privacy policy applies.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/starrlord/FallDesktop/issues>.
